# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis (SQL + Excel)

An end-to-end pizza sales analysis project using SQL Server and Excel to understand sales performance, customer ordering patterns, revenue contribution by pizza category and size, and identify the best and worst-selling pizzas.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The goal was to analyse pizza sales data using SQL queries, extract meaningful business insights and present the findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

The analysis covers key performance indicators (KPIs), daily and hourly order trends, sales distribution by pizza category and size, and best and worst-selling pizzas.

**Dataset:** Pizza sales records containing fields such as order ID, order date, order time, pizza name, pizza category, pizza size, quantity and total price.

---

## 🤔 Business Questions

### ❓ 1. What are the key sales performance indicators?

The business generated approximately **$817.86K in total revenue** from 21,350 orders, selling 49,574 pizzas. The average order value is $38.31, with approximately 2.32 pizzas sold per order.

### ❓ 2. Which days generate the highest number of orders?

Friday is the busiest day with 3,538 orders, followed by Thursday (3,239) and Saturday (3,158). Sunday records the lowest order volume with 2,624 orders.

### ❓ 3. What are the busiest hours for pizza orders?

Order activity is highest during lunch hours, particularly between 12 PM and 2 PM, and during the evening peak between 5 PM and 7 PM. The highest hourly order count occurs at 12 PM with 2,520 orders.

### ❓ 4. Which pizza categories contribute the most to revenue?

Classic pizzas lead with approximately 26.91% of total revenue, followed by Supreme (25.46%), Chicken (23.96%) and Veggie (23.68%). Classic is also the leading category in terms of total pizzas sold, with 14,888 pizzas.

### ❓ 5. Which pizza sizes contribute the most to sales?

Large pizzas dominate with approximately 45.89% of total revenue, followed by Medium (30.49%), Regular (21.77%) and X-Large (1.72%). XX-Large contributes only 0.12%.

### ❓ 6. Which are the best and worst-selling pizzas?

The top three best-selling pizzas by quantity are:

- The Classic Deluxe Pizza – 2,453 pizzas
- The Barbecue Chicken Pizza – 2,432 pizzas
- The Hawaiian Pizza – 2,422 pizzas

The three worst-selling pizzas are Brie Carre, Mediterranean and Calabrese, with 490, 934 and 937 pizzas sold respectively.

### ❓ 7. Which pizzas perform best within each category?

The best-selling pizzas in each category are:

- **Chicken:** The Barbecue Chicken Pizza (2,432)
- **Classic:** The Classic Deluxe Pizza (2,453)
- **Supreme:** The Sicilian Pizza (1,938)
- **Veggie:** The Four Cheese Pizza (1,902)

## 💡 Recommendations

1. **Optimise peak-hour operations:** Ensure sufficient staff and kitchen capacity during lunch (12 PM–2 PM) and evening (5 PM–7 PM) rush hours.
2. **Focus on high-performing categories:** Prioritise Classic pizzas while maintaining a balanced offering across Supreme, Chicken and Veggie categories.
3. **Promote large pizzas:** Use combo deals and promotional offers to maximise revenue from the most popular pizza size.
4. **Plan staffing around demand:** Allocate resources based on daily order patterns, particularly the higher demand on Fridays and Thursdays.
5. **Leverage best-selling products:** Use popular pizzas such as Classic Deluxe and Barbecue Chicken in promotional campaigns and combo offers.
